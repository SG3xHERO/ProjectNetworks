Get Playing

Up & Running in Minutes

Four steps from download to playing with the community

01

Download & Install

Grab the installer above, run it, and Project Launcher installs to your PC. Takes about 30 seconds.

02

Sign in with Microsoft

Log in with your real Minecraft account via secure Microsoft OAuth. Your account, your skin, your achievements.

03

Pick a Season Pack

The launcher pulls the latest server-managed modpacks automatically. Pick the current season and hit install.

04

Hit Play

Mods install, Java launches, you're in. See who's online right in the launcher before you even join.

Requirements

What You'll Need

Minimum
  • OS: Windows 10 64-bit
  • RAM: 8 GB system (6 GB to Minecraft)
  • Java: Bundled — no install needed
  • Storage: 5 GB free
  • Account: Minecraft Java Edition
Recommended Best Experience
  • OS: Windows 11 64-bit
  • RAM: 16 GB system (8 GB to Minecraft)
  • GPU: Dedicated graphics card
  • Storage: SSD with 10 GB+ free
  • Internet: Broadband connection
Good to Know
  • Java is bundled with the launcher — you don't need to install it separately
  • The launcher manages RAM allocation per pack automatically
  • Updates to packs and the launcher itself happen silently in the background
